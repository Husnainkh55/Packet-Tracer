# Packet-Tracer
Vlan Switching
<br>
Connect 6 PC's with a Switch <br>
<br> Configuration of IP addresses <br> 
<br> PC1 & PC 2 >>>>> VLAN 10 "IT" <br>
<br> PC3 & PC 4 >>>>> VLAN 20 "HR" <br>
<br> PC5 & PC 6 >>>>> VLAN 30 "Finance" <br>
<br> Creating Vlan's 10,20,30 Respectively <br>
<br> Using this Syntax <br> 
Switch(config)# vlan 10
Switch(config-vlan)# name IT
Switch(config-vlan)# exit
